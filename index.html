About ORAN

One parent company. Many brands.

ORAN International Ventures Pvt Ltd is the parent company of a growing family of brands. Each brand has its own identity and audience, and all share the same commitment to quality, professional expertise and honest ingredients.

From Orangewood's salon-quality repair and smoothing ranges to Biotop India's precision-driven professional care, our brands are made to perform in the salon chair and at home.
